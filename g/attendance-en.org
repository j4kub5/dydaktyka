* Attendance
- Attendance at classes is mandatory.
- The attendance form is available at: https://forms.office.com/e/rDKUfQhKgN
- *IMPORTANT*: always remember to complete the attendance form on the day of the class.
- Failure to complete the form on the day of the class has the same effect as not responding when attendance is checked orally.

** Absences
Two absences are allowed. Each additional absence lowers the final grade to the next lower grade on the applicable grading scale.
