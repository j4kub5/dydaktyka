* Rules for our classes
- Discussion is allowed (and welcome!), talking outside of discussion is not.
- Disturbing the class is prohibited.
- Photographing people and recording sound are prohibited.
- Violating the above rules may result in receiving a *yellow card*. Every *two subsequent yellow cards result in the grade being lowered* to the next lowest level.
- Be kind to others.
- Discussions regarding politics are permissible only as an exception, provided they serve the educational purpose of the class. Otherwise, I kindly ask that political topics not be raised.
- Be cautious when advising others.

** Special yellow card rule
Failure to include a yellow card in the activity form will result in the grade being lowered to the next lower grade.
