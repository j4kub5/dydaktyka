* Rules for our classes
- I encourage you to take part in the discussion. Please do not have side conversations.
- Please do not disrupt the class.
- Please do not photograph people or record audio.
- Violating the above rules may result in receiving a *yellow card*. Every *two subsequent yellow cards result in the grade being lowered* to the next lowest level.
- Be kind.
- Discussions regarding politics are permissible only as an exception, provided they serve the educational purpose of the class. Otherwise, I kindly ask that political topics not be raised.
- Be cautious when advising others.

** Special yellow card rule
Failure to include a yellow card in the activity form will result in the grade being lowered to the next lower grade.

** Questions and contacting the instructor
- Before asking a question, check the syllabus and the materials provided.
- Before emailing me or asking a question during a break, consider whether the answer will also be relevant to others. If so, please ask the question in the group discussion during class or in the General channel of the course team in Teams, so that others can also benefit from the answer.
